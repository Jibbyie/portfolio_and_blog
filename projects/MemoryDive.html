My contribution

Building the complete gameplay loop.

As the solo programmer on a three-person team, I built the player movement, collection, oxygen, hiding, enemy behavior, UI, and game-flow systems needed to turn the concept into a complete playable jam project.

Technical highlights

Systems that coordinate tension.

  • A central GameManager coordinates win/loss conditions, collection updates, memory text, audio cues, and timed enemy spawning.
  • Separated player movement, stats, hiding, and collection responsibilities so the one-week codebase could be debugged and extended quickly.
  • Used state-based enemy behavior to switch between patrol and pursuit based on whether the player was hidden.
  • Built an oxygen resource loop with replenishment and threshold-based UI and audio feedback.

Development challenges

Challenge → approach → result

  • Many systems, one week: Centralised game flow and separated player responsibilities so core systems could be integrated without turning the jam build into one monolithic script.
  • Readable tension: Combined oxygen thresholds, hiding state, timed spawning, and audio/UI feedback to make the threat legible to the player.
  • Team integration: Worked with the artist and composer to integrate assets and audio into a playable loop under the deadline.

Development decisions

Simple states, clear signals.

The project prioritised explicit player states and event-driven reactions over a larger framework. That kept the implementation understandable while allowing collection, oxygen, hiding, and enemy encounters to react consistently.

Outcome

A complete one-week playable build.

Memory Dive delivered a finished stealth-horror game loop for the Unpolished Game Jam, with collection, resource management, hiding, enemy pressure, and win/loss states working together in a playable release.
