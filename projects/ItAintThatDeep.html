About the Project

It Ain’t That Deep is a stylized 3D underwater exploration game created as part of our MSc thesis in Creative Digital Media & UX. Co-developed with Jake Avra (Designer), it combines narrative, systems design, and accessible interface theory to guide players through an atmospheric marine world.

The game revolves around environmental storytelling and the subtle communication of player objectives through carefully layered feedback, blending dialouge based interaction, quests, immersive UI, and iterative UX research.

Technical Highlights

  • Third-person underwater movement with curved momentum controls
  • Diegetic + HUD UI hybrid design based on player cognition and clarity
  • Systemic design to reward exploration without explicit markers
  • Figma prototypes fully mapped to responsive Unity UI systems

Development Focus

  • Testing how UX and sensory design can improve clarity and immersion
  • Prototyping accessibility-minded systems for non-verbal communication
  • Building responsive controls and interfaces for fluid underwater play
  • Blending creative narrative beats with player-driven discovery
  • Balancing environmental storytelling with actionable feedback

What We Learned

  • How to apply academic UX frameworks to in-game systems
  • Building a vertical slice that integrates design, systems, and player testing
  • Collaborating on a scoped but ambitious project with research goals
  • Creating interface clarity without tutorials or dialogue
  • Translating Figma prototypes into Unity-ready, layered UI flows
